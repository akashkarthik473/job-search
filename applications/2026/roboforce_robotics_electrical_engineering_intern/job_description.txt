RoboForce — Robotics Electrical Engineering Intern

RoboForce develops Physical AI-powered robots for demanding industrial work.
The internship bridges hardware design and embedded software by bringing
in-house electronics boards to life.

Responsibilities:
- Perform circuit validation, including power-on, signal-integrity, and
  continuity tests
- Troubleshoot hardware using oscilloscopes, multimeters, and logic analyzers
- Develop, test, and maintain Arduino C++ and STM32 C firmware using open-source
  libraries
- Implement and debug CAN, I2C, SPI, and UART
- Validate communication protocols in Linux environments
- Ensure firmware reliability and robustness in real-world conditions
- Turn debugging and firmware details into clear, actionable engineering guides
