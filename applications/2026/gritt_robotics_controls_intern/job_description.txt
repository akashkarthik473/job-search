Gritt — Robotics Controls Internship

Interns own a scoped deliverable end-to-end, work with a mentor, and demo the
result to the full team. Duration is either 3 or 6 months.

Work:
- Develop motion-planning or control algorithms and take them to hardware
- Benchmark against baselines and tune for robustness in real-world conditions
- Integrate with perception and learning components in the full stack

Qualifications:
- MS/PhD in Robotics, ME, EE, or related field preferred; strong BS candidates welcome
- Foundations in dynamics, optimization, and control theory
- Strong C++ or Python
- Robot hardware, competition-team, or research experience
- Ownership with light supervision and strong problem-solving skills
- US work authorization for a 3- or 6-month internship

Nice to have: MPC, whole-body control, sampling/optimization-based planning, or
related techniques. Example projects include safe-stop/human-avoidance around
live crews and manipulation from a moving base on rough terrain.
