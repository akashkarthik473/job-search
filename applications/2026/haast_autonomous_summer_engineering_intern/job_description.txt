ABOUT

Haast Autonomous is building an autonomous aircraft network for time-sensitive
medical logistics. Developing long-range VTOL aircraft, autonomy software, and
the operational foundation needed to move critical payloads between hospitals,
labs, and healthcare facilities on-demand, faster and more reliably.

"We're early, small, and moving quickly. Joining now means you'll have a direct
role in shaping the foundation of Haast — our culture, engineering process,
testing discipline, partner relationships, and the path from prototype to a real
medical logistics network."

"We're looking for high-agency people who want ownership, urgency, and
system-level impact from day one."

ABOUT THE ROLE

Haast is seeking Summer Engineering Interns to join our aircraft development
team. This is a hands-on opportunity for students eager to work directly with
autonomous aircraft — not just observe, but actively contribute to real hardware
and engineering decisions. Interns will collaborate closely with the founding
engineering team across aircraft design, prototyping, avionics, simulation,
manufacturing, and flight-testing.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

- Support the design, build, and testing of autonomous aircraft prototypes.
- Work in one or more key areas: airframe design, mechanical design,
  prototyping, avionics, controls, simulation, manufacturing, or flight-test
  support.
- Build, assemble, modify, and repair aircraft hardware.
- Create CAD models, test fixtures, documentation, analysis, or simulation tools
  according to your background.
- Assist with flight-test preparation, data review, repairs, and design
  iteration.
- Work directly with engineers to turn test results into tangible improvements.
- Own a meaningful project that directly contributes to our aircraft development
  program.

YOU MAY BE A FIT IF YOU ARE

- Pursuing a degree in aerospace, mechanical, electrical, robotics, computer
  engineering, computer science, or a related technical field.
- Excited about aircraft, drones, autonomy, hardware, or medical logistics.
- Hands-on and willing to work in a shop, hangar, or field-test environment.
- Comfortable learning quickly and navigating ambiguity.
- Practical, self-directed, and ready to take ownership.
- Eager to work in an early-stage startup where priorities shift quickly and the
  work is meaningful.

NICE TO HAVE

- Experience with CAD, 3D printing, drones, aircraft, robotics, controls,
  simulation, electronics, or manufacturing.
- Participation in student projects like rocketry, FORMULA SAE, robotics, UAV
  teams, design teams, research labs, or personal hardware projects.
- Familiarity with Python, MATLAB, SolidWorks, ArduPilot, PX4, electronics,
  composites, or flight testing.
- Experience building something that had to actually work in the real world.

"We care more about agency, ownership, taste, and evidence that you can build
than whether you match every bullet point. If you have worked on aircraft,
drones, robots, vehicles, rockets, or other hard technical systems and want to
help build something from the ground up, we want to hear from you."
