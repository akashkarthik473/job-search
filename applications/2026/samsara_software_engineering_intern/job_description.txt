ABOUT SAMSARA

Samsara is the pioneer of the Connected Operations(TM) Cloud, a platform that
enables organizations that depend on physical operations to harness Internet of
Things (IoT) data to develop actionable insights and improve their operations.
Industries served: agriculture, construction, field services, transportation,
manufacturing — more than 40% of global GDP.

Product areas: Video-Based Safety, Vehicle Telematics, Apps and Driver
Workflows, Equipment Monitoring.

ABOUT THE ROLE

Interns work across a range of engineering challenges depending on team:

- Backend infrastructure powering the IoT platform; scaling data ingestion and
  storage systems supporting over 15,000 hardware assets and tens of thousands
  of customers worldwide.
- Full stack: intuitive user experiences that turn data into actionable
  insights, partnering with product and design to iterate rapidly.
- Mobile: infrastructure behind the React Native app, enabling feature teams to
  build performant, maintainable products.
- Embedded software: software running directly on Samsara hardware — edge AI,
  multimedia frameworks, power management, and real-time data processing in
  demanding real-world environments.

Throughout the interview process they dig into past experience, what you want to
learn next, and where you'd best fit across engineering teams.
