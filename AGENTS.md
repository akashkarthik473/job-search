# Resume Writing Rules

These rules apply to every resume and application artifact in this repository.

* Do not use em dashes or en dashes in rendered resume text.
* Do not use hyphens as sentence, clause, or title separators. Rewrite the sentence or use commas, periods, parentheses, or colons.
* Keep hyphens when they are grammatically required in compound terms or literal names, such as real-time, four-motor, software-in-the-loop, and SR-Wireless-CAN.
* Avoid semicolons. Use one only when it is clearly more readable than splitting or rewriting the sentence.
* Before exporting a resume, search the TeX content and extracted PDF text for em dashes, en dashes, and semicolons. Review each remaining hyphen and keep it only when the phrase requires it.
